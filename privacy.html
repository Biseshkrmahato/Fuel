FuelIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

What FuelIQ is

FuelIQ is a personal vehicle fuel, trip and expense tracker that runs entirely in your web browser.

Data we store

The entries you log (fuel fills, trips, expenses, vehicles, reminders) are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. We do not operate a server and we do not receive, collect or sell your data.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, FuelIQ asks for permission to see your Google account email address and basic profile, and to create and manage only the files it creates in your Drive (the drive.file scope). It uses this to save a backup file of your FuelIQ data to your own Drive and to read it back on your other devices. Your data goes directly between your browser and Google; it never passes through any server operated by us.

Your email address is used only to show which account is connected. FuelIQ's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Receipt scanning

If you scan a receipt, the image is processed on your device by an OCR library running in your browser. The image is not uploaded anywhere.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer your data to third parties.

Your control

You can delete your data at any time by clearing the site's storage in your browser and by deleting the FuelIQ backup file from your Google Drive. You can revoke FuelIQ's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
